Why this matters

Slow endpoint responses hold up the delivery worker for longer than expected, reducing throughput for all deliveries in the batch. If response times continue to exceed {{ThresholdMs}}ms, future deliveries may be marked as failed and enter the retry queue — eventually reaching the Dead Letter Queue if not resolved.

Things to check on your endpoint

  • Your server is not under unusually high load or CPU pressure.
  • Database queries triggered by the webhook handler are not running slowly.
  • Your endpoint acknowledges the webhook quickly (return 200 first, process async).
  • No network latency between your server and the webhook service.
  • Timeout settings on your server are not causing the delay.
